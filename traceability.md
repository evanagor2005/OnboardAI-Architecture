## Consistency review

- Which SOW deliverables have no software requirements?
- Which software requirements have no verification method?
- Which architecture rules have no source requirement or approved constraint?
- Which design choices cannot be traced to a requirement or rule?
- Which accepted rules are missing from the design or tests?
