# Prototype acceptance criteria

Specify what evidence will show that the statement of work has been delivered. Detailed methods and cases belong in [`../verification/`](../verification/README.md).

## Questions

- Which user journeys must work end to end?
- Which requirements are acceptance gates, and which are evaluation goals?
- What results are needed for answer grounding, citations, access control, and onboarding usefulness?
- Who reviews and accepts the results?
- How will known limitations and incomplete requirements be reported?
