# Software requirements

Define **what** the prototype shall do and the conditions it shall meet, without selecting its internal design. Derive requirements from the [statement of work](../sow/statement-of-work.md). Each accepted requirement needs a stable ID, one testable obligation, a source, a priority, and a verification method. Mark scope as prototype, future, or both.

## Requirement format

| ID | Shall statement | Source / use case | Priority | Scope | Verification method | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Statuses: Proposed, Accepted, Deferred, Superseded. Do not treat a proposed statement as an approved requirement.
