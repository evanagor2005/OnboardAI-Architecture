# Security and privacy requirements

## Questions

- Who shall be allowed to see or change each kind of content and user data?
- What behavior shall be consistent across the employee interface, administration, and MCP tools?
- What shall happen when identity or permission information is missing or uncertain?
- What information shall be protected in logs, generated answers, citations, and external services?
- What audit, retention, deletion, and incident information shall the system provide?
- Which threats or misuse cases must the prototype withstand or demonstrate defenses against?
