# Quality attributes and constraints

Define measurable qualities for the prototype. Do not invent thresholds; the team must choose them or mark them open.

## Questions

- What accuracy and citation quality are required for useful answers?
- What responsiveness is acceptable for the main user journeys?
- What availability and recovery are needed for a credible demonstration?
- What accessibility and usability outcomes are required?
- What limits apply to budget, operating cost, data size, team skill, and schedule?
- What maintainability, portability, and dependency risks matter to the team?
