# Knowledge and data requirements

## Questions

- Which categories of organizational knowledge shall the prototype use?
- What source identity, version, owner, date, and location shall remain visible or retrievable?
- How shall content additions, corrections, removals, and permission changes affect system behavior?
- What shall happen when sources conflict, are outdated, or are unavailable?
- What data about users, conversations, progress, and evaluations shall be retained or deleted?
- What data volumes and update frequencies must the prototype support?
