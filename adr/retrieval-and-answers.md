- What material shall be eligible for retrieval for a specific user?
- What source evidence shall accompany an answer or claim?
- When shall the system abstain, express uncertainty, or ask for clarification?

- What shall be recorded so answer quality can be evaluated and reproduced?
- What constraints shall apply to external model services and transmitted data?
