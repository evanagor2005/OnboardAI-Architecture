# Architecture rules — identity and access

## Questions

- What identity context shall every protected request carry?
- What authorization checks shall occur before content or derived information is returned?
- How shall access outcomes remain consistent across UI, APIs, retrieval, and MCP tools?
- What shall happen when the policy or permission information is unavailable?
- Which actions and denials shall be auditable?
- What shall prevent cached or indexed material from bypassing access rules?
