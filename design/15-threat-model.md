# Threat model

Use the accepted scope, data inventory, system map, and access rules. Focus on credible prototype risks and the evidence needed to show the defenses work.

## Questions

- What sensitive information and critical functions must be protected?
- Where do trust boundaries and external inputs enter the system?
- How might a user or source cause unauthorized disclosure, misleading answers, or unauthorized actions?
- Which controls prevent or detect each misuse case?
- What residual risks are accepted for the prototype, and by whom?
